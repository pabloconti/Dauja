# Compact contact banner image

AI-generated port scene for the DAUJA prototype, based on the client-provided design reference. It is an illustrative scene, not a documentary photograph of company facilities.

Asset: assets/images/puerto-cta-premium.webp
Composition: port at dusk, reach stacker lifting a DAUJA container, white truck with red container. No UI baked into image. Text and contact button remain accessible HTML. DAUJA lettering corrected to corporate red throughout at the client's request.

Layout: compact 164px desktop band with horizontal text / photo / button composition. A single subtle four-second light pass plays on entering the viewport; prefers-reduced-motion disables it. Mobile uses a compact 290px composition.
